Terms of service

The basics for using the NYCPPRC website, the member app, and our events.

Last updated October 26, 2026.

Agreement

These terms cover the NYC Philosophy and Psychology Readers Community (NYCPPRC) website, the member app, and the events we organize. If you use the site or the app, or you join an event, you agree to these terms.

The community

NYCPPRC is a reading community in New York City, online and in person, running since November 2019. Ryan Bohman organizes it. It is not yet a registered nonprofit. Questions go to ryan@nycpprc.org, to ryan@being.art, or through the contact form.

Accounts

The public site is open without an account. The member app needs a sign-in, either with Google or with an email link. Use your own account, and give us information that is accurate. You are responsible for what happens under your sign-in. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms.

Conduct

Discuss ideas, and treat people decently. No harassment, slurs, threats, or hate. No spam or unwanted promotion. Do not share another person's private information. Do not record a discussion unless the people there have agreed. Hosts and moderators may remove posts and suspend accounts. The same expectations apply in the member app, including rooms and direct messages.

Events and RSVPs

If you RSVP, please attend, or cancel if your plans change, so the seat and the discussion match who is actually coming. Read the assigned material when the session asks you to. Follow the host's plan for that meeting. Online and in-person events are discussions among members.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you write and post. You allow NYCPPRC to display it on the site and in the app so the community can run. We may remove content that breaks these terms. The NYCPPRC name, logo, and website text belong to the community. Do not use them in a way that suggests you speak for the group.

Donations

Giving is optional. Donations go directly to Ryan Bohman, the organizer, personally, to cover the costs of running the group. NYCPPRC is not yet a registered nonprofit, so donations are not tax-deductible. Payments are processed by Pledge on Meetup. Details are on the donate page. This website does not charge dues and does not take card payments itself.

The site and app

We provide the site and app as they are. We may change, pause, or remove features. Member comments and readings are the views of the people who share them.

Contact

Email ryan@nycpprc.org or ryan@being.art, or use the contact form. The privacy policy explains what we do with Google sign-in data.
