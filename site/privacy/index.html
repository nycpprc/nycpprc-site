Privacy policy

How Gnosis Events handles information, including Google account data when you sign in.

Last updated October 28, 2026.

Who we are

This policy covers the Gnosis Events website and member app: the site at nycpprc.org and the app at nycpprc.org/app. gnosis.events is the upcoming address for Gnosis Events. This policy covers that domain when it points at this site.

NYCPPRC, the NYC Plato & Philosophy Reading Club, is the group on this site. On Meetup the group is listed as the NYC Philosophy and Psychology Readers Community. Ryan Bohman organizes it. The group has met online and in New York City since November 2019. NYCPPRC is not a registered nonprofit.

Contact

Email ryan@nycpprc.org. You can also email ryan@being.art, which is the contact address in the member app, or use the contact form.

Google sign-in

The member app can sign you in with Google, using Supabase Auth. If you choose Google, we receive the basic account details Google shares for that sign-in: your name, your email address, and your profile picture, when Google provides them. Those come from the openid, email, and profile permissions.

We do not request access to your Google contacts, calendar, Drive, or other Google data.

The app can also sign you in with an email link. If you use that, we receive the email address you enter.

How we use it

We use this information to create your member account and to sign you in. We show your name and profile picture in the community app. We use your email to reach you about your account, membership, or events when we need to.

We use Google user data only for those features. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored in Supabase, on the project that serves api.nycpprc.org. Account and sign-in emails are sent with Resend from auth.nycpprc.org.

Sharing

We do not sell Google user data, or other personal information. We share information only as needed to run the site and app, with the providers that host those services (including Supabase and Resend), or if the law requires us to.

Other information on the website

You can use the public website without a Google account.

  • If you join the newsletter, Mailchimp stores the email address you submit and sends those messages.
  • Contact and host forms store the message you send.
  • The site uses Google Analytics to count visits.
  • Event pages, RSVPs, and the Meetup group live on Meetup, under Meetup's own terms.
  • Optional donations are described on our donate page.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep member account data, including the Google profile details we stored, for as long as the account is open. To delete your account, email ryan@nycpprc.org or ryan@being.art and ask us to delete it. We will delete the account data we hold, unless we have to keep a particular record because the law requires it.

You can also remove this app’s access in your Google Account settings. It may be listed there as NYCPPRC. That stops future sign-in through Google. It does not erase an account we already created, so email us if you want the account deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

This page describes what we actually do. It is not legal advice.
