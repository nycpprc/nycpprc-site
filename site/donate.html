Donate to NYCPPRC

If the readings and discussions have meant something to you, you can chip in to help keep the NYC Plato & Philosophy Reading Club going on Gnosis Events.

Donate on Meetup

Please read before you give: donations go directly to Ryan Bohman, the organizer, personally, to cover the costs of running the group. NYCPPRC is not yet a registered nonprofit, so donations are not tax-deductible.

Payments are processed by Pledge on Meetup. The button opens the group’s Meetup page, where the donate box appears.

Giving is completely optional. Every event stays open to everyone either way, and showing up, reading along, and bringing a friend help just as much.

Questions about donations? Get in touch.

Gnosis Events

A home for reading clubs and discussion groups, sibling to Gnosis Health. NYCPPRC, the NYC Plato & Philosophy Reading Club, has met online and in New York City since November 2019.

nycpprc.org remains the home of NYCPPRC. gnosis.events is the upcoming home for Gnosis Events.
