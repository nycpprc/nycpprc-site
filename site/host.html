Host an event

Host an event with NYCPPRC on Gnosis Events. Whether it's your book club or if you want to give a talk.

Beware of scams: NYCPPRC and Gnosis Events will never ask you to pay to host an event. People have been pretending to be the club and asking hosts for money. Please don’t pay anyone who claims to be from NYCPPRC or Gnosis Events. If you’re unsure whether a message is really from us, get in touch.
