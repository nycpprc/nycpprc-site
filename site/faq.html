FAQ

Questions about NYCPPRC, the NYC Plato & Philosophy Reading Club, on Gnosis Events.

What is Gnosis Events, and what is NYCPPRC?

Gnosis Events is the platform for this club, with more groups planned. It is a sibling of Gnosis Health. NYCPPRC is the NYC Plato & Philosophy Reading Club, the group on this site. The club has been reading and discussing together since November 2019, online and in New York City. On Meetup the group is listed as the NYC Philosophy and Psychology Readers Community. nycpprc.org stays this group’s address. gnosis.events is the upcoming address for Gnosis Events.

Why did this meetup start?

We created this meetup in November 2019. Our goal was to read books about philosophy and psychology and discuss them together. Along with the goal of making new friends. We started as an in-person meetup, meeting in Manhattan, NYC. When COVID-19 occurred we pivoted to online meetups. Now we run both online and in person.

What is the goal of the meetup?

The goal of the meetup is to better understand what the author is saying. It is an act of empathy with the text.

How can I best participate in the meetup?

Do The Reading — This Meetup group is a reading group, not a lecture or seminar group. Please read the assigned reading before participating. That is the point of this group, we read then discuss what we read. Sometimes we listen to lectures or videos instead of reading, so when we request you do the reading, it also refers to doing the listening or watching if required. Reading means you read it this week, not years ago.

Be Inclusive — We have found that participation by everyone in the meeting who has read creates the most beautiful experience. We would like to include you in the conversation if you have read.

How can I give you feedback or contact the organizer?

You can contact us here or also send the organizer a message on Meetup. We would love to hear from you, especially if you have feedback on the group or a book suggestion or want to collaborate.

Are you affiliated with any group, belief system or movement?

No. We are not affiliated with any belief system, political party, religion or movement.

Are these recorded?

No recording please. Please respect privacy by not sharing what other people say at meetup.
